Student-First Features

Designed for the real college routine

Every component is engineered around student psychology: zero shame, zero friction, maximum peace of mind.

Smart Recalibration

Dynamic Daily Spending Ring

If you skip coffee today, that ₹50 automatically rolls into your tomorrow or weekend buffer. No math needed.

Speed & Simplicity

3-Tap Lightning Entry

Tap Chai, Rickshaw, or Photocopy. No manual typing, no 10-step forms. Logged before your tea gets cold.

100% Privacy

Zero Bank SMS Access

We never ask for your bank logins, UPI access, or SMS permissions. All your finances stay safely offline on your device.

Overspend Shield

Weekend Protection

Hostel Living

Quick Roommate Bill Split
